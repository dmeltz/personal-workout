// Personal Workout exercise library.
// Format: see LibraryParser in core. Each exercise is a ladder of variations, easiest first.
// "start" is the variation a new exercise begins on (1 = first). "intro" orders when exercises join
// the plan: all intro 1 exercises are in the first week, the rest come in one at a time.
// Ranges are per set (reps, or seconds). One-sided moves ("sides: each" or "sides: alternate", or the same
// at the end of a variation line) always count per side: 8 means 8 on the left and 8 on the right.
// The goal is muscle build-up, so most ranges sit around 6-15 reps, and harder variations replace endless reps.

// ───────────────────────────── PUSH ─────────────────────────────

@exercise PUSHUP
title: Push-up
group: push
measure: reps
sets: 2
rest: 60
start: 2
intro: 1
figure: PUSHUP
muscles: chest triceps shoulders | abs wrists
cue: Body in one straight line from head to heels: squeeze glutes and brace your belly.
cue: Hands a little wider than shoulders, elbows about 45° from your body, not flared out.
cue: Lower until your chest is a fist from the floor, then push the floor away.
variation: Incline push-up | 8-15 | Hands on a sturdy table, counter or windowsill. The higher the surface, the easier.
figure: PUSHUP_INCLINE
variation: Push-up | 6-15 | Hands on the floor.
figure: PUSHUP
cue: If your hips sag, rest and finish the set on a table instead.
variation: Slow push-up | 6-12 | 3 seconds down, 1 second pause just above the floor, then push up.
figure: PUSHUP_SLOW
variation: Feet-elevated push-up | 6-12 | Feet on a chair or step, hands on the floor.
figure: PUSHUP_FEET_UP
cue: Keep your hips in line: the raised feet make them want to pike up.
variation: Archer push-up | 3-6 | Hands very wide. Lower toward one hand while the other arm stays almost straight, then push up and lower toward the other hand. | alternate
figure: ARCHER_PUSHUP

@exercise PIKE
title: Pike push-up
group: push

sets: 2
rest: 60
start: 1
intro: 2
figure: PIKE

cue: Hips high so your body makes an upside-down V. Most of your weight goes on your hands.
cue: Lower the top of your head toward a spot a little in front of your hands, elbows pointing back, not out.
cue: Push back up until your arms are straight. Shoulders do the work.
variation: Pike push-up | 5-12 | Feet on the floor, walked in close to your hands.

variation: Slow pike push-up | 5-12 | 3 seconds down, then push up.
figure: PIKE_SLOW
variation: Feet-elevated pike push-up | 5-10 | Feet on a chair, hips stacked as high above your shoulders as you can.
figure: PIKE_FEET_UP
cue: Start with your knees bent if your hamstrings are tight.

@exercise CLOSE_PUSHUP
title: Close-grip push-up
group: push
measure: reps
sets: 2
rest: 60
start: 1
intro: 4
figure: CLOSE_PUSHUP
muscles: triceps chest | shoulders abs wrists
cue: Hands shoulder-width or a little closer, directly under your shoulders.
cue: Elbows brush your ribs on the way down. This moves the work to the triceps.

figure: DIAMOND_PUSHUP
cue: If your wrists or elbows complain, go back to the close-grip version.

@exercise HANDSTAND
title: Handstand
group: push
measure: seconds
sets: 2
rest: 60
start: 1
intro: 1
equipment: a clear stretch of wall
figure: HANDSTAND
muscles: shoulders triceps wrists | upper_back abs forearms hands
cue: Arms locked straight. Push the floor away so your shoulders reach up toward your ears.
cue: Hands shoulder-width, fingers spread, and grip the floor with your fingertips to stay balanced.
cue: Ribs in and glutes squeezed so your body is one straight line, not a banana.
cue: To come down, step down the way you went up, or turn one hand and cartwheel out (your capoeira aú).
variation: Wall L-hold | 15-40 | Hands on the floor about a leg's length out from the wall, feet against it. Walk your feet up the wall to hip height and walk your hands in until your hips are over your shoulders, body in an L.
figure: HS_WALL_L
variation: Chest-to-wall handstand | 15-45 | Start in a push-up position with your feet at the wall. Walk your feet up the wall and your hands in until your chest almost touches it, then hold.
figure: HS_CHEST_WALL
cue: Walk down the same way. Your hands end a hand's width from the wall.
variation: Back-to-wall handstand | 15-45 | Hands about a hand's length from the wall. Kick up one leg at a time and let your heels rest on the wall, then hold.
figure: HS_BACK_WALL
cue: Take your heels off the wall for a second or two at a time when the hold feels easy.
variation: Freestanding handstand | 5-20 | Kick up away from the wall, on grass or near the wall as a backstop, and balance on your hands. Count your best hold of each try.
figure: HANDSTAND
