// Personal Workout exercise library.
// Format: see LibraryParser in core. Each exercise is a ladder of variations, easiest first.
// "start" is the variation a new exercise begins on (1 = first). "intro" orders when exercises join
// the plan: all intro 1 exercises are in the first week, the rest come in one at a time.
// Ranges are per set (reps, or seconds). Per-side exercises: the range is per side.
// The goal is muscle build-up, so most ranges sit around 6-15 reps, and harder variations replace endless reps.

// ───────────────────────────── PUSH ─────────────────────────────

@exercise PUSHUP
title: Push-up
group: push
measure: reps
sets: 2
rest: 60
start: 2
intro: 1
figure: PUSHUP
muscles: chest triceps shoulders | abs
cue: Body in one straight line from head to heels: squeeze glutes and brace your belly.
cue: Hands a little wider than shoulders, elbows about 45° from your body, not flared out.
cue: Lower until your chest is a fist from the floor, then push the floor away.
variation: Incline push-up | 8-15 | Hands on a sturdy table, counter or windowsill. The higher the surface, the easier.
variation: Push-up | 6-15 | Hands on the floor.
cue: If your hips sag, rest and finish the set on a table instead.
variation: Slow push-up | 6-12 | 3 seconds down, 1 second pause just above the floor, then push up.
variation: Feet-elevated push-up | 6-12 | Feet on a chair or step, hands on the floor.
cue: Keep your hips in line: the raised feet make them want to pike up.
variation: Archer push-up | 4-10 | Hands very wide. Lower toward one hand while the other arm stays almost straight. Alternate sides; count each side as one rep.

@exercise PIKE
title: Pike push-up
group: push
measure: reps
sets: 2
rest: 60
start: 1
intro: 2
figure: PIKE
muscles: shoulders triceps | upper_back
cue: Hips high so your body makes an upside-down V. Most of your weight goes on your hands.
cue: Lower the top of your head toward a spot a little in front of your hands, elbows pointing back, not out.
cue: Push back up until your arms are straight. Shoulders do the work.
variation: Pike push-up | 5-12 | Feet on the floor, walked in close to your hands.
variation: Slow pike push-up | 5-12 | 3 seconds down, then push up.
variation: Feet-elevated pike push-up | 5-10 | Feet on a chair, hips stacked as high above your shoulders as you can.
cue: Start with your knees bent if your hamstrings are tight.

@exercise CLOSE_PUSHUP
title: Close-grip push-up
group: push
measure: reps
sets: 2
rest: 60
start: 1
intro: 4
figure: PUSHUP
muscles: triceps chest | shoulders abs
cue: Hands shoulder-width or a little closer, directly under your shoulders.
cue: Elbows brush your ribs on the way down. This moves the work to the triceps.
cue: Body stays in one straight line, same as a normal push-up.
variation: Close-grip incline push-up | 8-15 | Hands on a table or counter.
variation: Close-grip push-up | 6-12 | Hands on the floor.
variation: Diamond push-up | 5-12 | Index fingers and thumbs touch to make a diamond under your chest.
cue: If your wrists or elbows complain, go back to the close-grip version.

// ───────────────────────────── PULL ─────────────────────────────

@exercise DEAD_HANG
title: Hang from the bar
group: pull
measure: seconds
sets: 2
rest: 60
start: 2
intro: 1
equipment: pull-up bar
figure: HANG
muscles: forearms lats | shoulders
cue: Overhand grip, hands a little wider than your shoulders, thumbs around the bar.
cue: Breathe normally. Stop the set when your grip starts to slip, not after.
variation: Supported hang | 20-45 | Feet on a chair under the bar, carrying some of your weight.
variation: Dead hang | 20-60 | Hang with your whole weight, arms straight and shoulders relaxed.
variation: Active hang | 20-50 | Pull your shoulders down away from your ears without bending your arms, and hold that.
cue: Your body rises a few centimeters when you pull your shoulders down. That's the move.
variation: Flexed-arm hang | 10-30 | Jump or step up so your chin is over the bar, and hold it there.

@exercise PULLUP
title: Pull-up
group: pull
measure: reps
sets: 2
rest: 90
start: 2
intro: 1
equipment: pull-up bar
figure: PULLUP
muscles: lats upper_back biceps | forearms abs
cue: Overhand grip, a little wider than your shoulders.
cue: Start each rep by pulling your shoulders down, then drive your elbows down toward your hips.
cue: Lower all the way to straight arms under control. No swinging or kicking.
variation: Scapular pull-up | 6-12 | Hang with straight arms and only pull your shoulders down and back, then relax. Small movement.
variation: Negative pull-up | 3-6 | Step up on a chair so your chin is over the bar, then lower yourself as slowly as you can, about 5 seconds.
cue: Step back up for each rep. Don't jump into the next one.
variation: Pull-up | 2-8 | Full pull-ups from a dead hang until your chin is over the bar.
cue: Can't get one more full rep? Finish the set with slow negatives and log the full reps only.
variation: Slow pull-up | 3-8 | 2 seconds up, a short pause with your chin over the bar, 3 seconds down.

@exercise CHINUP
title: Chin-up
group: pull
measure: reps
sets: 2
rest: 90
start: 1
intro: 3
equipment: pull-up bar
figure: PULLUP
muscles: biceps lats | upper_back forearms
cue: Underhand grip (palms toward you), hands shoulder-width apart.
cue: Pull your chest toward the bar, elbows close to your sides. Biceps work harder than in pull-ups.
cue: Lower all the way to straight arms under control.
variation: Negative chin-up | 3-6 | Step up on a chair with your chin over the bar, then lower yourself in about 5 seconds.
variation: Chin-up | 2-8 | Full chin-ups from a dead hang.
variation: Slow chin-up | 3-8 | 2 seconds up, a short pause at the top, 3 seconds down.

@exercise TOWEL_ROW
title: Door towel row
group: pull
measure: reps
sets: 2
rest: 60
start: 1
intro: 2
equipment: a towel and a sturdy door
figure: ROW
muscles: upper_back lats biceps | forearms lower_back
cue: Loop a towel around both handles of a closed door (or a post in the yard). Hold one end in each hand.
cue: Feet close to the door, lean back with straight arms and a straight body.
cue: Pull your chest toward the door, squeezing your shoulder blades together, then lower slowly.
cue: Check that the door is closed and latched, and pull on its handle side only if it opens away from you.
variation: Upright towel row | 8-15 | Lean back only a little. Walk your feet back to make it easier.
variation: Towel row | 8-15 | Walk your feet closer to the door so you lean back further.
variation: Slow towel row | 8-15 | Lean back as far as you control. Pause 2 seconds at the top of each rep.
variation: One-arm towel row | 5-10 | Hold both towel ends in one hand, other hand on your chest. Do all the reps on one side, then the other.

@exercise SQUAT
title: Squat
group: legs
measure: reps
sets: 2
rest: 60
start: 2
intro: 1
figure: SQUAT
muscles: quads glutes | hamstrings lower_back
cue: Feet shoulder-width, toes turned out a little. Arms forward for balance.
cue: Sit back and down, knees pointing the same way as your toes. Keep your heels down.
cue: Chest up, back neutral. Go as deep as you can while your heels stay down and your back stays straight.
variation: Chair squat | 10-20 | Sit down to a chair, touch lightly without resting, and stand up.
variation: Squat | 12-25 | Bodyweight squat, thighs at least parallel to the floor.
variation: Pause squat | 10-20 | Hold the bottom position 3 seconds on every rep.
variation: Cossack squat | 6-12 | Very wide stance. Sit down over one leg while the other stays straight, toes up. Alternate sides; count each side.
variation: Assisted pistol squat | 4-10 | On one leg, holding the door frame lightly. Sit back to a low chair. Count each leg.

@exercise SPLIT_SQUAT
title: Split squat
group: legs
measure: reps
sets: 2
rest: 45
start: 2
intro: 2
per side: yes
figure: SPLIT
muscles: quads glutes | hamstrings calves
cue: Long stride: front foot flat, back heel up. Most of your weight on the front leg.
cue: Lower straight down until your back knee almost touches the floor. Torso upright.
cue: Front knee follows the toes. Push up through the front heel.
variation: Supported split squat | 8-15 | Hold a wall or chair for balance.
variation: Split squat | 8-15 | No support.
variation: Bulgarian split squat | 6-12 | Back foot on a chair or couch behind you.
cue: Hop your front foot forward if your front knee travels far past your toes.
variation: Slow Bulgarian split squat | 6-12 | Back foot raised, 3 seconds down every rep.

@exercise GLUTE_BRIDGE

group: legs
measure: reps
sets: 2
rest: 45
start: 2
intro: 1
figure: BRIDGE
muscles: glutes hamstrings | lower_back
cue: Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat near your hips.
cue: Push through your heels and lift your hips until your body is straight from knees to shoulders.

cue: The reps count per leg.
variation: Single-leg hip thrust | 8-15 | Shoulders on the couch or a chair, one foot on the floor. Do both legs.

@exercise STEP_UP

figure: STEPUP

@exercise BAG
title: Bag rounds
group: conditioning
measure: seconds
sets: 2
rest: 45
start: 1
intro: 2
equipment: boxing bag
figure: BAG
muscles: shoulders | obliques chest triceps
cue: Stand side-on, hands up guarding your chin, knees soft. Keep moving your feet.
cue: Punch with your whole body: turn your hip and shoulder into it, and bring the hand straight back to your face.
cue: Breathe out on every punch. Wrap or glove your hands so your wrists stay straight.
variation: Steady round | 30-60 | Jab and cross at a steady pace.
variation: Moving round | 30-75 | Jab, cross and hooks, moving around the bag.
variation: Power round | 30-60 | Hard combinations of 3-4 punches, short rest inside the round.

// ───────────────────────────── SAFETY ─────────────────────────────

@safety
line: Stop the exercise if anything hurts: sharp pain, joint pain, or pain that gets worse as you go. Normal muscle burn is fine; pain is not.
line: Stop and rest if you feel dizzy, short of breath beyond normal effort, or chest pain. Get medical help for chest pain.
line: Skip or ease off an exercise when a joint is sore, and tap "Skip / couldn't do it". The app will ease that exercise off.
line: Good form first: fewer clean reps build more muscle than many sloppy ones.
